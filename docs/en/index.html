FAQ

Usage notes

Can this content guarantee commercial metrics?

No. Numeric design needs target-user data, regional context, instrumentation, and repeated A/B testing. This page makes no revenue, retention, or payment claims.

Why is “complete fishing source” not the primary target?

This repository’s distinct evidence is numeric documentation, gameplay screenshots, Lua entry points, and Proto3 protocols. The companion repository covers source and art.

Who should use this reference?

Teams researching fishing-game balance, gameplay configuration, Unity Lua integration, and Protobuf structures.
